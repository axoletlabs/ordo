# Changelog

## 0.3.0 Preview 5

### Smoother every day

Moving through Ordo feels quicker, especially with a large library. This update also makes it easier to pick up reading where you left off.

- **Smoother controls**
  Menus, dialogs, pickers, and reader controls respond more smoothly.
- **Quicker library actions**
  Searching, moving bookmarks, and deleting items feel more responsive in large libraries.
- **A more reliable reading position**
  Reading progress is more accurate, and returning to an article restores your position more reliably.

#### Smaller fixes

- Keyboard focus is more reliable when switching between dialogs.
- Bookmark rows have tighter spacing and smoother selection feedback.

## 0.3.0 Preview 4

### More control over your library

Deleting a folder no longer has to mean losing the bookmarks inside it. Small app updates also need less attention from you.

- **Keep the bookmarks you want**
  Choose which bookmarks to keep when deleting a folder.
- **Quieter updates**
  Small updates install the next time you open the app.

#### Other improvements

- Sign-in and account settings are more reliable.
- Updates and release history use clearer version names.

## 0.3.0 Preview 3

### Less waiting, more reading

Everyday interactions feel smoother, and the reader gives your article's contents more room.

- **Quicker reader controls**
  Reader controls open faster, with more space in the Contents panel.
- **Smoother navigation**
  Menus and dialogs feel smoother as you move around the app.

#### Fixes

- If a tag edit fails, the previous tags are restored.

## 0.3.0 Preview 2

### Your updates, explained

Release notes now appear after an update, with earlier updates available to browse. This preview also improves reminders, backup codes, and article spacing.

- **Your updates in one place**
  Read the latest changes after updating, or look back through earlier releases.
- **Easier backup codes**
  Save backup codes to a file. Cancelling the save no longer shows an error.

#### Everyday improvements

- Custom reminder pickers open faster, and bookmark selection is steadier.
- Articles have less empty space at the end.

## 0.2.0

### A fresh look for reading

Ordo has a refreshed design, faster library search, and more ways to make reading comfortable.

- **Colors that feel at home**

  Choose from new reading fonts, adjust line spacing, and move through articles more easily.

## 0.2.0 Preview 2

### Clearer names, quicker moves

It's easier to tell your Preview and Development apps apart, and moving bookmarks no longer adds an extra wait.

- **Know which app you're opening**
  Preview and Development have clearer app names.
- **Move and carry on**
  Bookmarks move immediately, without an extra closing delay.

#### Before you update

Updating from Preview 1 requires uninstalling it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cache are cleared.

## 0.2.0 Preview 1

## 0.1.0

### Your reading, in one place

Save the links you want to return to, keep them organized, and read at your own pace.

- **Save from other apps**
  Send links to Ordo, or use one-tap Quick Save.
- **Make your library yours**
  Organize bookmarks with folders and tags, and lock private folders.
- **Pick up where you left off**
  Read with themes and highlights while Ordo saves your progress.

#### More for your library

- Search your bookmarks, set reminders, and import or export your collection.
- Use Ordo Cloud or connect to your own server.
