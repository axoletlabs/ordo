# Changelog

## 0.3.0 Preview 5

- Smoother menus, dialogs, pickers, and reader controls.
- More responsive search, bookmark moves, and deletion in large libraries.
- More reliable keyboard focus when switching between dialogs.
- More accurate reading progress and restored reading positions.
- Tighter bookmark rows and smoother selection feedback.

## 0.3.0 Preview 4

- Choose which bookmarks to keep when deleting a folder.
- Small updates install quietly the next time you open the app.
- More reliable sign-in and account settings.
- Clearer version names in updates and release history.

## 0.3.0 Preview 3

- Smoother menus, dialogs, and everyday interactions.
- Faster reader controls and more room in the Contents panel.
- Failed tag edits now restore the previous tags.

## 0.3.0 Preview 2

- See what's new after an update, with easier-to-read release notes and earlier updates.
- Save backup codes to a file; cancelling no longer shows an error.
- Faster custom reminder pickers and steadier bookmark selection.
- Less empty space at the end of articles.

## 0.3.0 Preview 1

- Faster reading, smoother large libraries, and smaller downloads.
- More reliable bookmark moves and deletion undo, even after restarting.
- Android Back cancels an incoming share.
- A smaller Contents panel, clearer reminder dates, and easier sign-in code editing.

## 0.2.0

- Refreshed design with Android wallpaper colors.
- Faster search and smoother bookmark organization.
- New reading fonts, adjustable line spacing, and easier navigation.
- More reliable sign-in, reminders, and app updates.

Preview users: install the Stable app and sign in to access your library. Local settings stay in Preview.

## 0.2.0 Preview 2

- Clearer Preview and Development app names.
- Bookmarks move immediately without an extra closing delay.

Updating from Preview 1 requires uninstalling it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cache are cleared.

## 0.2.0 Preview 1

- A refreshed Material design with optional Expressive controls and Android wallpaper colors.
- One library search bar, faster search, and cleaner bookmark lists.
- An immersive reader with five fonts, line spacing, highlights, and easier section navigation.
- Easier reminders, more accessible dialogs, and more reliable sign-in and updates.

Preview installs alongside the Stable app. Sign in to access your library; local settings are separate.

## 0.1.0

- Save links from other apps, including one-tap Quick Save.
- Organize with folders and tags; lock private folders.
- Read with themes, highlights, and saved progress.
- Search your library, set reminders, and import or export bookmarks.
- Use ordo Cloud or your own server.
