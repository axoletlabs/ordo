### Smoother everyday actions

This Preview improves library actions and makes it easier to return to your place in an article.

#### Improved

- **Library actions** — Search, move bookmarks, and delete items with less waiting.
- **Controls** — Menus, dialogs, pickers, and reader controls open more smoothly.

#### Fixed

- Reading progress is more accurate, and articles return to your saved place more reliably.
- Keyboard focus returns to the right field more reliably after switching dialogs.

#### Before you update

If you are updating from 0.2.0 Preview 1 on Android, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
