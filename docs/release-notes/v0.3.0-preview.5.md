### Smoother every day

This Preview makes library actions and reader controls more responsive, especially in large libraries.

#### Improved

- Search, move bookmarks, and delete items with less waiting.
- Menus, dialogs, pickers, and reader controls open more smoothly.
- Bookmark rows use space more neatly while keeping their touch targets.

#### Fixed

- Reading progress is more accurate, and articles return to your saved position more reliably.
- Keyboard focus returns to the right field more reliably when switching dialogs.
- The search field no longer keeps its focus outline after focus moves away.
