### Save several links and edit titles

This Preview lets you save more than one bookmark at a time and change a bookmark's title.

#### New

- **Save several links** — Paste, share, or type more than one link and save them together. Add another link from the save sheet.
- **Edit a title** — Change a bookmark's title from its menu or while reading.
- **Auto-paste links** — In Controls, fill the save form from your clipboard, one field per link.

#### Improved

- **Bookmark rows** — Swipe a row to mark it read or delete it. Deleting still offers Undo.
- **App colors** — Presets sit on one row, and a custom color uses a color picker.
- **Sheets and pickers** — Bottom sheets follow your finger, and folder and tag pickers stay full height while the keyboard is open.
- **Toasts** — Swipe a message off the screen and it travels with your finger.
- **Website titles** — Pages that are not articles keep a real title when one is available.

#### Fixed

- Pulling to refresh the library no longer fights other menus.
- The in-app browser can overscroll again on Android.
- Back in the reader starts from the screen edge.
- Active sessions can be pulled to refresh.

#### Before you update

If you are updating from 0.2.0 Preview 1 on Android, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
