### Your updates, explained

This Preview shows release notes after an update and improves a few everyday tasks.

#### Improved

- **Release notes** — See what's changed after an update and browse earlier releases.
- **Reminders and reading** — The custom reminder calendar opens with less waiting, and articles have less empty space at the end.

#### Fixed

- Canceling a backup-code download no longer shows an error.

#### Before you update

If you are updating from 0.2.0 Preview 1 on Android, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
