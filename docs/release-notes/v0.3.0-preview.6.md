### Fewer ads, your own colors

This Preview adds browser ad blocking, custom app colors, and private tools for checking performance.

#### New

- The built-in browser blocks ads and ad redirects by default. Add sites to exceptions when needed.
- Save color palettes and choose custom app colors. The AMOLED option is now next to the theme choice.
- Run a performance check or make a diagnostic report. Reports stay on your device unless you choose to export them.
- View open-source licenses and build details in About without a network connection.

#### Improved

- Menus, dialogs, switches, and loading indicators move more smoothly in both design modes.
- Release notes are easier to read and release selection is simpler.
- The folder deletion picker shows the bookmark count and opens more quickly.

#### Fixed

- Selected bookmarks stay safe when their folder is deleted.
- Page loading progress stays at the top of the page.
