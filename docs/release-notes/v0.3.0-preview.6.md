### Fewer ads, your own colors

This Preview adds browser ad blocking, custom app colors, and tools to help diagnose problems.

#### New

- **Block ads** — The built-in browser blocks ads and ad redirects. Allow ads on sites that need them.
- **Choose app colors** — Pick a preset palette or create one from a custom color.
- **Get help with a problem** — Create a report or run a performance check on Android. Reports stay on your device until you choose to share them.
- **View licenses offline** — Find open-source licenses and build details in About without a connection.

#### Improved

- **Folder deletion** — The keep picker shows the bookmark count and opens with less waiting.
- **Release notes** — The notes and release selector are easier to use.
- **Controls** — Menus, dialogs, switches, and loading indicators move more smoothly.

#### Fixed

- Bookmarks selected to keep stay safe when their folder is deleted.
- Page loading progress stays at the top of the page.

#### Before you update

If you are updating from 0.2.0 Preview 1 on Android, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
