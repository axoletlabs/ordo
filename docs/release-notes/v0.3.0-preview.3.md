### Easier reminders and reading

This Preview adds an unread marker and makes reminders, reading, and sign-in setup easier to use.

#### New

- **Unread marker** — A dot on the site icon marks bookmarks you haven't read.

#### Improved

- **Reminder time** — Set a custom time with a clock picker.
- **Sign-in setup** — Copy your sign-in setup key or enlarge its QR code.
- **Reading** — Reader controls open with less waiting, and Contents gives article sections more room.
- **Release history** — Browse Stable notes alongside Preview history in a new sheet.

#### Fixed

- Failed tag edits restore the previous tags.
- Touches no longer reach the screen behind an open dialog.

#### Before you update

If you are updating from 0.2.0 Preview 1 on Android, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
