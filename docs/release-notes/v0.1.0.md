### Your reading, in one place

Save links, organize your library, and read at your own pace.

#### Save and organize

- Share links to Ordo from other apps, or use Quick Save.
- Group bookmarks with folders and tags. Lock folders you want to keep private.
- Search your library, set reminders, and import or export your bookmarks.

#### Read your way

- Read articles with themes and highlights, and return to your saved position.

#### Connect

- Use Ordo Cloud or connect to your own server.
