### More control over your library

This Preview lets you keep selected bookmarks when deleting a folder and makes small updates quieter.

#### New

- **Keep bookmarks** — Choose which bookmarks to keep when you delete a folder.

#### Improved

- **Quieter updates** — Small updates download in the background and take effect the next time you open Ordo.
- **Release history** — Version names are clearer.

#### Fixed

- Sign-in and account settings handle more cases reliably.

#### Before you update

If you are updating from 0.2.0 Preview 1 on Android, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
