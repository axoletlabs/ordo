### Easier reading and library actions

This Preview improves long articles and large libraries, and fixes some everyday actions.

#### Improved

- **Reading** — Long articles are more responsive, and the Contents panel takes less space and has a close button.
- **Library** — Scrolling large libraries is smoother, and reminder dates and sign-in code fields are clearer.

#### Fixed

- Moving bookmarks and undoing deletion work more reliably, including after a restart.
- Android Back can cancel an incoming share, and the Contents button no longer covers the end of an article.

#### Before you update

If you are updating from 0.2.0 Preview 1 on Android, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
