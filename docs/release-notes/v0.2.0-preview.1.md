### A fresh look for reading

This Preview refreshes the app and gives you more control over how you read.

#### New

- **Make Ordo yours** — Choose Expressive controls or use your Android wallpaper colors.
- **Line spacing** — Adjust the space between lines in the reader.

#### Improved

- **Reading** — Choose from updated reading fonts and use a clearer Contents panel and highlight controls.
- **Library search** — Search from one library bar and find matches even when accents differ.
- **Everyday controls** — Menus, lists, reminders, sign-in, and update screens are easier to use.

#### Changed

- The Sepia reader theme and Mono font are no longer available.

#### Fixed

- Saved articles no longer remain stuck loading after processing.
- Device lock no longer asks twice when you unlock the app.

#### Installing Preview

Preview installs alongside Stable. Sign in to use your library; settings on this device are separate.
