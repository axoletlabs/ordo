### A new look for Ordo

This first Preview brings a refreshed design and more ways to read.

#### New

- Choose Expressive controls or use your Android wallpaper colors in the app.
- Pick from five reading fonts, adjust line spacing, and manage highlights in the reader.
- Open Contents while reading and jump to a section of the article.

#### Improved

- Search from one library bar and find results faster, even without accents.
- Move around the app with smoother menus, lists, and selection controls.
- Set custom reminders with a simpler picker.
- Sign in and update the app more reliably, with clearer forms and larger controls.

#### Fixed

- Saved articles no longer stay stuck in a loading state after being processed.
- Device lock no longer asks twice when you unlock the app.

#### Installing Preview

Preview installs alongside Stable. Sign in to use your library; settings on this device are separate.
