### More control, smoother reading

Ordo 0.3.0 adds ad blocking, more app colors, and a way to keep bookmarks when deleting a folder.

#### New

- **Block ads** — The built-in browser blocks ads and ad redirects on Android and iOS. Allow ads on sites that need them.
- **Choose app colors** — Pick a preset palette or create one from a custom color.
- **Keep bookmarks** — Choose which bookmarks to keep when you delete a folder.
- **Unread marker** — A dot on the site icon marks bookmarks you haven't read.
- **Get help with a problem** — Create a report or run a performance check on Android. Reports stay on your device until you choose to share them.
- **View licenses offline** — Find open-source licenses and build details in About without a connection.

#### Improved

- **Reminder time** — Set a custom time with a clock picker.
- **Release notes** — See what's changed after an update and browse past releases.
- **Quieter updates** — Small updates download in the background and take effect the next time you open Ordo.
- **Library and reading** — Search and library actions need less waiting, long articles are more responsive, and Contents is easier to use.
- **Sign-in setup** — Copy your sign-in setup key, enlarge its QR code, and edit sign-in digits more easily.

#### Fixed

- Moving bookmarks and undoing deletion work more reliably, including after a restart.
- Bookmarks selected to keep stay safe when their folder is deleted, and failed tag edits restore the previous tags.
- Reading progress and returning to a saved place are more reliable.
- Canceling a backup-code download no longer shows an error, and Android Back can cancel an incoming share.

#### Before you update

If you are updating from 0.1.0 on Android, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
