### More control, smoother reading

Ordo 0.3.0 adds ad blocking, more app colors, and a safer way to delete folders. Reading and everyday actions are smoother too.

#### New

- **Block ads** — The built-in browser blocks ads and ad redirects on Android and iOS. You can allow ads on sites that need them.
- **Choose app colors** — Choose a preset palette or create one from a custom color.
- **Keep bookmarks** — Choose which bookmarks to keep when you delete a folder.
- **Spot unread bookmarks** — A dot on the site icon marks bookmarks you haven't read.
- **Get help with a problem** — Create a report or run a performance check on Android. Logs stay on your device until you choose to share them.
- **View licenses offline** — Find open-source licenses and build details in About without a connection.

#### Improved

- **Reminder picker** — Choose a custom reminder time using a clock.
- **Release notes** — See the notes after an update and browse past releases in a clearer view.
- **Quieter updates** — Small updates download in the background and take effect the next time you open Ordo.
- **Faster library** — Search, move bookmarks, delete items, and scroll through large libraries with less waiting.
- **Smoother reading** — Long articles and reader controls respond more smoothly. The Contents panel is easier to use.
- **Smoother controls** — Menus, dialogs, switches, and loading indicators move more smoothly.
- **Sign-in setup** — Copy your setup key, enlarge its QR code, and edit sign-in digits more easily.
- **Smaller downloads** — App downloads take up less space.

#### Fixed

- Moving bookmarks and undoing deletion work more reliably, including after a restart.
- Bookmarks you choose to keep stay safe when their folder is deleted. Failed tag edits restore the previous tags.
- Reading progress is more accurate, and articles return to your saved place more reliably.
- Keyboard focus returns to the right field more reliably after switching dialogs.
- Canceling a backup-code download no longer shows an error.
- Android Back can cancel an incoming share, and page loading progress stays at the top.
