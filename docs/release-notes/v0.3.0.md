### More control, smoother reading

Ordo 0.3.0 adds browser ad blocking, custom colors, and more control over your library. It also makes reading and everyday actions smoother.

#### New

- **Block ads:** The built-in browser blocks ads and ad redirects on Android and iOS. You can allow ads on sites that need them.
- **Choose your colors:** Save app color palettes and pick your own colors.
- **Keep bookmarks:** Choose which bookmarks to keep when you delete a folder.
- **Set reminder times:** Use the new clock to choose a custom reminder time.
- **See what's unread:** A badge on each site's icon marks unread bookmarks.
- **Read update notes:** See what changed after an update, or browse earlier releases in About.
- **Get help with a problem:** Create a report or run a performance check. Logs stay on your device until you choose to share them.
- **View licenses offline:** Find open-source licenses and build details in About, even without a connection.

#### Improved

- Large libraries scroll more smoothly, and search, moving bookmarks, and deletion respond faster.
- Long articles open and scroll more smoothly. The Contents panel is easier to open and gives sections more room.
- Menus, dialogs, switches, and loading indicators move more smoothly across the app.
- Small app updates download quietly and take effect the next time you open Ordo.
- Save backup codes to a file, copy your sign-in setup key, or enlarge its QR code.
- Sign-in code fields are easier to edit, and reminder dates are clearer.
- App downloads are smaller.

#### Fixed

- Moving bookmarks and undoing deletion are more reliable, including after a restart.
- Selected bookmarks stay safe when their folder is deleted. Failed tag edits restore the previous tags.
- Reading progress is more accurate, and articles return to your saved place more reliably.
- Keyboard focus returns to the right field more reliably after switching dialogs.
- Sign-in and account settings handle more cases reliably.
- Android Back can cancel an incoming share, and page loading progress stays at the top.
