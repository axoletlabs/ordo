### A simpler move

This Preview removes a pause after moving bookmarks and makes app names clearer.

#### Improved

- **Move bookmarks** — Moving bookmarks no longer has an extra closing delay.
- **App names** — Preview and Development are easier to tell apart on your device.

#### Before you update

To update from Preview 1, uninstall it first. Your saved library stays on the server, but local settings and cached data are cleared.
